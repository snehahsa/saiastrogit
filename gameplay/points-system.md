# Points System

Earn points by **surviving longer** and **collecting pickups**. Every second alive adds score; specials spike it; asteroids shave it.

## Pickup tiers

Fly into a pickup to claim it. **Pickups add score only** — they do **not** refill health or oxygen.

| Pickup | Points | Rarity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blue gem** | +10 | Common |
| **Yellow star** | +25 | Uncommon |
| **Pink crown** | +60 | Rare |

## Passive survival

**+10 PTS** every second you stay alive.

That's the baseline grind — clock watching is part of the skill curve.

---

## Asteroid tax

Each asteroid hit: **−3 PTS**.

Dodge discipline beats greedy pickup routes every time.

---

## Score formula

```
PTS = (seconds alive × 10) + pickup bonuses − (asteroid hits × 3)
```

{% hint style="success" %}
**High-score mindset:** Ride survival ticks, hunt pink crowns when safe, and treat asteroids like a tax you refuse to pay.
{% endhint %}
