# Gameplay

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/gameplaybanner.png" alt="Astroverse X gameplay"><figcaption>Beta live — multiple modes, one universe.</figcaption></figure>

Fly in the Astroverse with a **virtual joystick** and **full 360° camera**, then drop into multiplayer where social play meets **P2E $ASTROVERSE** rewards.

Chat with your squad, boost past danger, strike in combat, and uncover hidden realms together.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**BETA** — Modes and balance ship fast. Timelines on the [Roadmap](../roadmap.md) are targets, not promises carved in stone.
{% endhint %}
