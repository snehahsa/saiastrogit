## Racing Mode

Racing Mode introduces high-speed competitive gameplay throughout the Astroverse.

Players compete across dangerous space tracks using skill, movement, timing, and speed mechanics to reach the finish line.

### Features

* Multiplayer racing
* Nitro boost systems
* Competitive leaderboards
* Time attack mode
* Seasonal tournaments
* High-speed space tracks

### Competitive Systems

Players can:

* Compete for leaderboard rankings
* Improve lap times
* Unlock achievements
* Participate in seasonal events

### Future Expansion

Future racing updates may include:

* Ranked mode
* Team racing
* Custom race maps
* Betting systems
* Spectator mode
* Tournament rewards
