<details>

<summary><strong>Will $ASTROVERSE be used in-game? Are there burns or buybacks?</strong></summary>

Yes. **$ASTROVERSE** powers the wider ecosystem.

In-game revenue is allocated to **burns**, **player rewards**, and **buybacks** through a **100% revenue share** model on **Base** — see [Revenue share](revenue-share.md).

See [Tokenomics](tokenomics.md) for allocation, **5 ETH LP**, and tax split.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Does it run on phone and desktop?</strong></summary>

Today it's a **browser-based game** on modern phones and desktop browsers.

A **Steam** release for Windows and macOS is planned as the experience grows.

Play: [app.astroversex.com](https://app.astroversex.com/)

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Do I need to connect my wallet to play?</strong></summary>

**No wallet connection** is required in the game client for core play.

Identity and eligibility can be verified through the **Telegram bot** — you don't need to connect a wallet in the web app just to start.

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Will the $ASTROVERSE contract be renounced and liquidity locked?</strong></summary>

Yes — **planned** as follows, subject to final **legal and security review**:

* **5 ETH liquidity pool** on **Base** — locked for **three years**
* **Contract renounce** immediately after launch

Always verify on official channels before trading.

</details>
