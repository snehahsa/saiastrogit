# Revenue share

{% hint style="success" %}
**100% revenue share.** Not a slice — the whole engine. Every stream the Astroverse opens — ads, launches, casinos, gameplay — is wired back to **buybacks**, **holders**, and **players**. Zero extraction. Full loop.
{% endhint %}

Game revenue goes to **buybacks**, **holder rewards**, and what players earn in-game.

***

## How revenue flows today

{% columns %}
{% column %}
### Buybacks

NFT ads, Astroverse launches, casino revenue, and more fund **automated buybacks** — tightening supply as the universe scales.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
### Holder share

Each day, the top **0.05%** of holders split tax fees. Snapshot time is **random**; payouts settle **on-chain**.
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}

{% columns %}
{% column %}
### Play to earn

**Real tokens in-game.** Earn from fights, quests, and completed runs — not points that die in the lobby.
{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}
### Tax flywheel

The **3/3 tax** on $ASTROVERSE routes one third each to in-game rewards & buybacks, marketing & partnerships, and development — see [Tokenomics](tokenomics.md).
{% endcolumn %}
{% endcolumns %}

## Futuristic goals

The revenue model is built to **compound** as the multiverse expands — not plateau at launch.

* **Casino & clinic economies** — Q3 locations become live revenue rails, not just scenery
* **Launchpad at scale** — every meme community that ships a branded world adds a new fee stream back to buybacks and holders
* **Degen Space (Q4)** — real-time on-chain social layer with integrated interaction and new monetization surfaces
* **Native VR (Q4)** — immersive sessions, premium placements, and ad bands in three dimensions
* **Expanded holder mechanics** — deeper daily snapshots, on-chain settlement, and transparent tax routing as volume grows
* **Supply discipline** — **5% buyback & lock** on the [Roadmap](roadmap.md) tightens float while revenue keeps cycling

{% hint style="info" %}
**The north star:** Play more → revenue rises → buybacks + holder payouts + P2E intensify → more communities launch → repeat. That's the **100% revenue share** promise — built for the long arc, not a one-season grind.
{% endhint %}
